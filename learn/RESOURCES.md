## Institutional background
- **Boone & Mulherin (2007), "How Are Firms Sold?"** (JF, high) — evidence that most takeover competition happens privately, before public bids. https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2007.01225.x
- **Imprivata DEFM14A (2016)** (SEC filing, primary) — a real "background of the merger" narrative showing approaches, outreach, indications of interest. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1328015/000119312516677939/d226798ddefm14a.htm
- **Gentry & Stroup (2019), "Entry and Competition in Takeover Auctions"** (JFE, high) — structural evidence on endogenous bidder entry. https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0304405X18302964

## Deterrence (the force the paper reverses)

- **Hirshleifer & Png (1989)** (RFS, high). https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article-abstract/2/4/587/1586915

## Feedback from prices to real decisions
- **Dow, Goldstein & Guembel (2017)** (JEEA, high) — investment decisions shape incentives to produce information. https://academic.oup.com/jeea/article/15/4/877/3054465
- **Edmans, Goldstein & Jiang (2012)** (JF, high) — prices affect takeover activity. https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2012.01738.x
- **Edmans, Goldstein & Jiang (2015)** (AER, high) — feedback and asymmetric trading. https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/aer.20141271
- **Goldstein & Guembel (2008)** (REStud, high) — manipulation when prices guide decisions. https://academic.oup.com/restud/article-abstract/75/1/133/1627011
