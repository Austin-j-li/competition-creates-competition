# 0001 Course design: five lessons, ELI5 first

**Date:** 2026-09-05
**Status:** accepted

## Context
Austin asked for a five-hour crash course covering intuition, mechanism, institutions, and full
proofs, at high-school-graduate level. An initial ten-lesson plan was rejected as too fragmented.

## Decision
Five lessons of about an hour: story and institutions; auction payoffs and Proposition 1; prices,
beliefs, and the investor's edge; Proposition 2 in full; certificates, robustness, welfare, design.
Each lesson opens with plain-words boxes and ends with quizzes and recall prompts.

## Insight to carry forward
The learner already knows the paper's conclusions. The zone of proximal development is the
machinery (posterior bounds, convolution derivative bound, interval certificates), not the story.
Later sessions should test derivations from memory, not comprehension of the narrative.
