# Mission

## Why I am learning this

I am the author of "Competition Creates Competition: Stock Prices and the Discovery of Takeover
Bidders" and I want to fully own it: the intuition, the economic mechanism, the institutional
background, and every mathematical derivation and proof. I want to be able to explain any
line of the paper to a skeptical seminar audience and to a friend with only high-school math.

## Starting point

Assume a high-school graduate: comfortable with algebra, basic probability, and the idea of a
derivative, but not with measure theory, Bayesian updating, or auction theory. Every lesson
explains new tools before using them (ELI5 first, then the real thing).

## Target

About five hours of guided study, in five lessons with breaks between sections, ending with the ability to:

1. Tell the story of the paper in plain words (one minute, no math).
2. Describe how a listed target is sold and where the "prepare or not" decision sits.
3. Derive every payoff in equation (4) and prove Proposition 1 from scratch.
4. Explain why noise bounds beliefs, why the price reveals the market's belief, and why the
   investor keeps an edge under rational pricing (equations (7) to (11)).
5. Reproduce the six-step proof of Proposition 2 and interpret conditions (A1) to (A3).
6. Explain what Proposition 3's computer-assisted certificate does and does not prove.
7. State the robustness, welfare, bargaining, and reserve results and their limits.

## Success looks like

Passing the synthesis quiz in lesson 5 without notes, and being able to rebuild the
formula sheet in `reference/formula-sheet.html` from memory.
