1. Primitives and assumptions

$$0\lt p\lt \ell\lt r\lt h \tag{1}$$

Reserve below the low value, low value below incumbent strength, strength below the high value.

$$q\{V_T-P(X)\}-k|q|,\qquad X=q+Z,\qquad f(z)=\frac{1}{2b}e^{-|z|/b},\quad b\gt 1 \tag{2}$$

Investor profit, order flow, and Laplace noise density.

$$P(X)=\mathbb E[V_T\mid X] \tag{3}$$

Competitive market makers set the price equal to the expected share payoff given flow.

2. Auction payoffs

$$\begin{aligned} t_0&=p\Big(1-\frac pr\Big), & t_H&=\frac r2+\frac{p^2}{2r}, & t_L&=\ell-\frac{\ell^2-p^2}{2r},\\ g_H&=h-\frac r2-\frac{p^2}{2r}, & g_L&=\frac{\ell^2-p^2}{2r}, & \Delta_T(r)&=\frac{(r-\ell)^2}{2r}. \end{aligned}\tag{4}$$

Expected proceeds without entry, with a high or low entrant; challenger gross profit by type; target-payoff spread.

$$B_r(\mu)=g_L+\mu(g_H-g_L),\qquad \Delta_T'(r)=\frac12-\frac{\ell^2}{2r^2}\gt 0,\qquad g_H'(r)=-\frac12+\frac{p^2}{2r^2}\lt 0,\qquad g_L'(r)=-\frac{\ell^2-p^2}{2r^2}\lt 0 \tag{5}$$

Profit at belief \(\mu\); a stronger incumbent widens the spread and lowers profit.

$$\Delta_T(F)=\mathbb E_F[(R-\ell)_+]=\int_\ell^{\bar r}[1-F(u)]\,du,\qquad G_\theta(F)=\mathbb E_F[(\theta-\max\{p,R\})_+]=\int_p^\theta F(u)\,du \tag{6}$$

Distribution-free spread and profit; FOSD raises the first, lowers the second (Proposition 1).

$$t_H=\frac1r\Big[\int_0^p p\,du+\int_p^r u\,du\Big],\quad t_L=\frac1r\Big[\int_0^p p\,du+\int_p^\ell u\,du+\int_\ell^r\ell\,du\Big],\quad g_L=\frac1r\Big[\int_0^p(\ell-p)\,du+\int_p^\ell(\ell-u)\,du\Big] \tag{A.1}$$

The integrals behind (4): pay the reserve, pay the rival's value, or pay the low value.

3. Beliefs and prices

$$a_\theta(x)=\int f(x-q)\,d\sigma_\theta(q),\qquad \mu_X(x)=\frac{a_H(x)}{a_H(x)+a_L(x)},\qquad m=\frac{1}{1+e^{2/b}},\quad M=1-m \tag{7}$$

Flow density by state, the posterior, and its floor and ceiling.

$$e^{-2/b}\le\frac{f(x-q)}{f(x-q')}\le e^{2/b}\quad\text{for all }q,q'\in[-1,1] \tag{8}$$

Bounded likelihood ratio: no order can move the posterior outside \([m,M]\).

$$e_r(\mu)=\rho+(1-\rho)\mathbf 1\{B_r(\mu)\ge c_H\},\qquad P_r(\mu)=t_0+e_r(\mu)[t_L-t_0+\Delta_T\mu] \tag{9}$$

Entry rule and the strictly increasing candidate price.

$$P=t_0+e(P)[t_L-t_0+\Delta_T\mu_X],\qquad \mu_X=\frac{P-t_0-e(P)(t_L-t_0)}{e(P)\Delta_T} \tag{10}$$

Price sufficiency: the challenger reads the market's belief off the price.

$$A_H(x)=e_r(\mu_X)\Delta_T[1-\mu_X],\qquad A_L(x)=e_r(\mu_X)\Delta_T\mu_X,\qquad \rho m\Delta_T\le A_H,A_L\le\Delta_T \tag{11}$$

Residual edge equals entry times spread times remaining uncertainty.

4. Proposition 2: conditions and outcomes

$$0\le c_L\lt B_{r_1}(m)\tag{A1}\qquad\qquad B_{r_0}(1/2)\lt c_H\lt B_{r_1}(M)\tag{A2}$$ $$\Delta_T(r_0)\lt k\lt \Big(1-\frac1b\Big)\rho m\Delta_T(r_1)\tag{A3}$$

Cheap entry always pays; expensive entry pays only after good news against the strong rival; trading cost sits between the weak-economy ceiling and the strong-economy floor.

$$\begin{aligned} \tau&=\frac{c_H-g_L}{g_H-g_L}, & x^*&=\frac b2\log\frac{\tau}{1-\tau},\\ \alpha_H&=1-\tfrac12e^{(x^*-1)/b}, & \alpha_L&=\tfrac12e^{-(x^*+1)/b},\\ \mathsf E&=\rho+\frac{1-\rho}{2}(\alpha_H+\alpha_L), & \mathsf O_H&=\tfrac12[\rho+(1-\rho)\alpha_H]. \end{aligned}\tag{12}$$

Threshold belief, threshold flow, crossing probabilities by state, total entry, and high-type ownership.

$$\mu_X(x)=\Big[1+\exp\Big\{-\frac{|x+1|-|x-1|}{b}\Big\}\Big]^{-1}\tag{A.8}$$

Posterior under full orders: \(m\) for \(x\le-1\), logistic\((2x/b)\) in between, \(M\) for \(x\ge1\).

$$\mathsf E(r)\to\rho+\frac{1-\rho}{4}(1+e^{-2/b})\quad\text{as }r\uparrow r_C\tag{A.14}$$

Entry just below the participation ceiling; above it, entry drops to \(\rho\).

5. Trading-bound machinery

$$F_H(s)=\int f(x-s)A_H(x)\,dx,\qquad F_L(s)=\int f(x+s)A_L(x)\,dx,\qquad U_\theta(s)=sF_\theta(s)-ks\tag{A.4}$$

Average edge and net profit of a correctly signed order of size \(s\).

$$U_\theta'(s)\ge\Big(1-\frac sb\Big)F_\theta(s)-k\ge\Big(1-\frac1b\Big)\rho m\Delta_T-k\gt 0\tag{A.6}$$

Under (A3) the strong-economy profit rises at every order size, so the full order is uniquely optimal.

$$J=F_H(1)=F_L(1)=\Delta_T\int e(x)\frac{f(x-1)f(x+1)}{f(x-1)+f(x+1)}\,dx,\qquad F_\theta(s)\ge e^{-(1-s)/b}J\tag{A.7}$$

Existence test for one fixed full-order candidate: \(k\lt(1-1/b)J\) makes every correctly signed marginal profit positive against that schedule only.

$$F(s_2)-F(s_1)=\int_{s_1}^{s_2}\!\!\int\partial_uf(x\mp u)A(x)\,dx\,du\tag{A.5}$$

Translation formula: \(F\) is absolutely continuous and \(|F'|\le F/b\).

$$s\Big(\frac{\rho\Delta_T(r)}2-k\Big)\tag{A.12}$$

Payoff of a deviation of size \(s\) against no trade; zero is optimal iff this is nonpositive.

6. Thresholds

$$\mathfrak r(d)=\ell+d+\sqrt{d^2+2\ell d},\qquad r_N=\mathfrak r(2k/\rho),\qquad r_U=\mathfrak r\Big(\frac{k}{(1-1/b)\rho m}\Big)\tag{A.10}$$

Strength at which \(\Delta_T=d\); no-trade existence boundary; full-order uniqueness bound. Uniqueness of no trade holds below \(\mathfrak r(k)\).

$$r_C=\frac{Mh-c_H+\sqrt{(Mh-c_H)^2+M[(1-M)\ell^2-p^2]}}{M}\tag{A.11}$$

Unique solution of \(B_r(M)=c_H\): the ceiling for expensive entry.

$$B_r(M)=Mh-\frac{Mr}2+\frac{(1-M)\ell^2-p^2}{2r}\tag{A.13}$$

Gross profit at the highest attainable belief; strictly decreasing in \(r\).

7. Certified equilibria (Proposition 3)

$$x^*(r,v)=\frac{b\,\mathrm{logit}(\tau)+1-v}{2},\qquad \mathsf E(r,v)=\rho+\frac{1-\rho}2\Big[1-\tfrac12e^{(x^*-1)/b}+\tfrac12e^{-(x^*+v)/b}\Big]\tag{A.15}$$

Threshold flow and entry under the asymmetric profile \((1,-v)\), with \(\mu_v=\mathrm{logistic}((|x+v|-|x-1|)/b)\).

$$F_L'(s)=-\int f(x+s)\,dA_L(x)\lt 0,\qquad |F_L''(s)|\le-\frac1bF_L'(s)\tag{A.16}$$

Stieltjes-measure derivative; gives \(U_L''\lt 0\), so the low type's problem is globally concave.

$$\Psi(r_j,v_{j,-})\gt 0,\qquad \Psi(r_j,v_{j,+})\lt 0\quad(j=\mathrm a,\mathrm b,\mathrm c)\tag{A.17}$$

Interval sign change of the low type's marginal profit at bracket endpoints; an exact root lies inside.

$$F_H''(s)=\frac{F_H(s)-A_H(s)}{b^2}\ \text{a.e.},\qquad |U_H''(s)|\le L_U:=\frac{2\Delta_T}b+\frac{\Delta_T}{b^2}\tag{A.18}$$

Lipschitz bound on the high type's marginal profit.

$$\inf_{s\in[0,1]}U_H'(s)\ge\min_j\underline{U_H'(s_j)}-\frac{L_U}{2n}\gt 0\tag{A.19}$$

Mesh cover: interval lower bounds at \(n+1\) grid points certify full purchases everywhere.

$$\text{primitives of }e^{\pm x/b}(1-\mu_v),\ e^{\pm x/b}\mu_v:\quad be^{c/b}\arctan t,\quad be^{-c/b}(-t^{-1}-\arctan t),\quad be^{c/b}(t-\arctan t),\quad be^{-c/b}\arctan t\tag{A.20}$$

Elementary antiderivatives with \(c=(1-v)/2\), \(t=e^{(x-c)/b}\); tails integrate as exponentials.

8. Logistic noise

$$f_{\log}(z)=\frac1{4b}\operatorname{sech}^2\Big(\frac z{2b}\Big),\quad b\gt 1\tag{A.21}$$

Alternative noise law; its log-density slope is also bounded by \(1/b\).

$$x^*_{\log}=b\log\frac{Aw-1}{A-w},\qquad \alpha_H^{\log}=\frac1{1+e^{(x^*_{\log}-1)/b}},\qquad \alpha_L^{\log}=\frac1{1+e^{(x^*_{\log}+1)/b}},\qquad A=e^{1/b},\ w=\sqrt{\tau/(1-\tau)}\tag{A.23}$$

Logistic threshold flow and crossing probabilities under full orders.

$$\log\frac{\mu}{1-\mu}=2\log\cosh\Big(\frac{x+1}{2b}\Big)-2\log\cosh\Big(\frac{x-1}{2b}\Big)\tag{A.22}$$

Logistic posterior log odds: increasing, limits \(\mp2/b\), bounds \(m,M\) reached only in the limit.

9. Atomless costs and nonemptiness

$$c_L-\varepsilon_C\ge0,\qquad c_L+\varepsilon_C\lt B_{r_1}(m),\qquad B_{r_0}(1/2)\lt c_H-\varepsilon_C\lt c_H+\varepsilon_C\lt B_{r_1}(M)\tag{A.24}$$

Support restrictions so every cheap realization enters and expensive ones enter only after good news against the strong rival.

$$B_\ell(M)-B_\ell(1/2)=(M-1/2)(h-\ell)\gt 0\tag{A.25}$$

At \(r=\ell\) the belief gap opens a cost window; strengths near \(\ell\) then satisfy (A1) to (A3) for any \(h\gt \ell\).

10. Complementary private signals

$$\Pr(T=+\mid H)=\Pr(T=-\mid L)=a,\qquad \Pr(Y=+\mid H)=\Pr(Y=-\mid L)=d,\qquad a,d\in(1/2,1)\tag{A.26}$$

Investor signal \(T\) with accuracy \(a\), buyer signal \(Y\) with accuracy \(d\), conditionally independent.

$$\mu_X=(1-a)+(2a-1)\lambda_X,\quad \mu_\pm=(1-a)+(2a-1)\{M,m\},\quad \phi_+(\mu)=\frac{d\mu}{d\mu+(1-d)(1-\mu)},\quad \phi_-(\mu)=\frac{(1-d)\mu}{(1-d)\mu+d(1-\mu)}\tag{A.27}$$

Public belief from the market's belief about \(T\); its bounds; the buyer's combined posteriors.

$$e_H=\rho+(1-\rho)[dI_++(1-d)I_-],\quad e_L=\rho+(1-\rho)[(1-d)I_++dI_-],\quad D=e_Hw_H-e_Lw_L\tag{A.28}$$ $$D=e_L\Delta_T+(e_H-e_L)w_H\in\big[\rho\Delta_T,\ \Delta_T+(1-\rho)(2d-1)w_H\big],\qquad D\ge\rho\Delta_T\gt 0\tag{A.33}$$

State-dependent entry with \(I_y=\mathbf 1\{B_r(\phi_y(\mu))\ge c_H\}\); the price coefficient \(D\) stays positive.

$$P=t_0+e_L(P)w_L+\mu_XD(P),\qquad \mu_X=\frac{P-t_0-e_L(P)w_L}{D(P)}\tag{A.34}$$

Pricing and inversion with private signals.

$$A_+=(2a-1)(1-\lambda_X)D,\qquad A_-=(2a-1)\lambda_XD\tag{A.35}$$ $$m(2a-1)\rho\Delta_T\le A_+,A_-\le(2a-1)[\Delta_T+(1-\rho)(2d-1)w_H]\tag{A.36}$$ $$U'(s)\ge(1-1/b)\,m(2a-1)\rho\Delta_T(r_1)-k\gt 0\tag{A.37}$$

Residuals by investor signal, their bounds, and the strong-economy derivative bound that forces full signal-contingent orders.

$$c_L\lt B_{r_1}(\phi_-(\mu_-)),\qquad B_{r_0}(d)\lt c_H\lt B_{r_1}(\phi_+(\mu_+)),\qquad (2a-1)\{\Delta_T(r_0)+(1-\rho)(2d-1)w_H(r_0)\}\lt k\lt \Big(1-\frac1b\Big)m(2a-1)\rho\Delta_T(r_1)\tag{A.29}$$

Proposition A.7's analogue of (A1) to (A3).

11. Welfare

$$W_\theta(R)-W_0(R)=(\theta-\max\{p,R\})_++p\mathbf 1\{R\lt p\}\tag{A.39}$$

Pointwise ownership-value gain from a challenger of value \(\theta\).

$$\Delta\mathcal W=(1-\rho)\mathbb E\Big[\Big(B_r(\mu_X)-c_H+\frac{p^2}r\Big)\mathbf 1\{\mu_X\ge\tau\}\Big]\gt 0,\qquad \Delta\mathcal R_T=\frac{1-\rho}2[\alpha_H(t_H-t_0)+\alpha_L(t_L-t_0)]\gt 0\tag{A.41}$$

Gains in acquisition surplus net of costs and in target proceeds from price access at fixed \(r_1\).

12. Bargaining

$$T_\eta(R,\theta)=(1-\eta)\min\{R,\theta\}+\eta\max\{R,\theta\},\qquad G_{\theta,\eta}=(1-\eta)\mathbb E[(\theta-R)_+],\qquad \Delta_\eta=\eta(h-\ell)+(1-2\eta)\mathbb E[(R-\ell)_+]\tag{14}$$

Nash bargaining with seller weight \(\eta\): competition widens the spread iff \(\eta\lt 1/2\).

13. Seller's continuation problem

$$\mathcal R_T(p,r;\sigma)=t_0(p,r)+\frac12\sum_{\theta}e_\theta(p,r;\sigma)[t_\theta(p,r)-t_0(p,r)],\qquad p^*\in\arg\max_{p\in[0,h]}\mathcal R_T(p,r;\sigma^*(p))\tag{A.44}$$

Revenue given a continuation, and the reserve problem given a selection from the continuation set.

$$t_0(p,r)=\mathbb E[p\mathbf 1\{R\ge p\}],\qquad t_v(p,r)=\begin{cases}\mathbb E[\max\{p,\min(R,v)\}],&v\ge p\\ t_0(p,r),&v\lt p\end{cases},\qquad g_v(p,r)=\mathbb E[(v-\max\{p,R\})_+]\tag{A.42}$$

Payoffs over the whole reserve domain.

$$\frac{d\mathcal R_T}{dp}=(1-\mathsf E)\Big(1-\frac{2p}r\Big)+\mathsf E\frac pr+\frac12\sum_\theta\frac{de_\theta}{dp}(t_\theta-t_0)\tag{A.53}$$ $$\frac{de_\theta}{dp}=\int f(z)h_C(B_{p,r}(\mu_\theta))\Big[-\frac pr+(g_H-g_L)\frac{d\mu_\theta(z;p)}{dp}\Big]dz\tag{A.54}$$

Local revenue decomposition: fixed-entry terms plus a participation term with a direct and an information component.

$$P_c(x)=\begin{cases}0,&x\lt c\\ \tfrac74\mu_X(x),&x\ge c\end{cases},\qquad \bar\mu_c=\Pr(H\mid X\lt c)=\frac{F_Z(c-1)}{F_Z(c-1)+F_Z(c+1)}\tag{A.45, A.46}$$ $$J_c\gt\frac{7}{32},\qquad U_\theta'(s)\gt\frac{7}{64}-\frac1{50}=\frac{143}{1600}\gt0\tag{A.48}$$

Price pooling at fixed orders (Proposition A.10): benchmark primitives, \(r=r_0\), reserve \(p=7\), full orders, any cutoff \(c\in[-\log2,0]\). Same orders, different prices, beliefs, and entry.

$$p_L=h-\frac{c_L}{m},\qquad p_H=\sqrt{2r\Big(h-\frac{c_H}{M}\Big)-r^2}\tag{A.50, A.51}$$

Exact reserve events: cheap-type floor with the incumbent excluded (at \(r_0\)); expensive-type indifference at the top belief with \(\ell\lt p\lt r\) (at \(r_1\)).

$$\mathsf E=\Pr(I=1),\quad \mathsf A=\Pr(I=1,V\ge p),\quad \mathsf S=\Pr(R\ge p\ \text{or}\ [I=1,V\ge p]),\quad \mathsf C_2=\Pr(R\ge p,I=1,V\ge p)\tag{A.52}$$

Preparation, admissible challenger, sale, and two admissible bidders; \(\mathsf S=\Pr(R\ge p)+\mathsf A-\mathsf C_2\) and \(0\le\mathsf C_2\le\mathsf A\le\mathsf E\le1\).
