## B6 | backup, Proposition 2 in full, page 1
Five steps. First, the bounds for every profile. Second, at the weak incumbent every nonzero order loses in expectation, so a quiet market is the only outcome. Third, at the strong incumbent every correct order pays under every profile and a wrong-signed order always loses, so the orders are full. Fourth, a buy gives belief $M$, which covers the high cost. Fifth, at the stronger-still incumbent trade still pays but $M$ no longer covers the high cost. The last line gives the ownership condition.

## B6b | backup, Proposition 2 in full, page 2
Under full orders class $H$ faces a buy with chance $\lambda+b$ and class $L$ with chance $b$. Entry for each class is $\rho$ plus the chance of a buy times one minus $\rho$. Averaging over the prior gives the entry in the table on slide 13.

## B7 | backup, coexistence in full
The six marked strengths and their roles. Before $r_A$ no informative market exists. On the interval from $r_A$ to $r_F$ the mix rate adjusts and entry rises. From $r_F$ to $r_N$ a full purchase coexists with a quiet market. Past $r_N$, the quiet market fails, and mixed orders at the entry floor join the full purchase until $r_J$. The low class sells for sure above $r_S$. The entry ceiling $r_C$ is where the top posterior stops short of the high cost.

## B8 | backup, research in full, page 1
Research picks $\lambda$ between zero and the cap before the slot. Zero research is an equilibrium up to $r_N$. Recruiting research is an equilibrium from $r_R$ to the entry ceiling: below the cap up to $r_{\bar\lambda}$, at the cap after it, with entry rising and then constant. Floor-entry research, with entry at $\rho$, is an equilibrium just above $r_N$ up to $r_Q$ and above the entry ceiling. There is no other equilibrium. At the three intermediate strengths there are exactly two equilibria, zero research and recruiting research below the cap. The unique outcome has zero research at the weak incumbent, recruiting research at the cap at the strong one, and floor-entry research at the cap at the stronger-still one.

## B10 | backup, sale terms in full
With price access the proceeds gain and the surplus gain are both positive at the strong incumbent. At a reserve that excludes the low class, entry is flat. The payment rule's spread response has the sign of one minus twice the seller's share. Strict deterrence needs a higher reserve that also excludes the weak incumbent.

## B11 | backup, order sizes
The slot uses unit orders so that every price is on path. A menu of continuous sizes needs a convention for sizes no class uses. With Laplace noise and full unit orders, the top posterior equals the slot value when $1+u=e^{2/\xi}$. The two set-ups then share the posterior bounds and differ in how often each class reaches the top.
