Austin's standing brief for proposal drafts (told to GPT Astra for the "Competition Creates Competition" upgrade proposal, 2026-09-05): framing loud and bold, limitations and caveats not at center stage, aim at top finance journals over one to two years, do not fear "overclaiming."

**Why:** The document is a second-year PhD upgrade manuscript plus presentation that must set an ambitious stage for one to two more years of work. When I critiqued the proposal without knowing this, I faulted deliberate framing choices (hedged abstract, trivial-claim removal, caveat placement) and Austin corrected me.

**How to apply:** When reviewing his proposals or drafts, separate substance from framing. Flag only what a referee or seminar would stop on regardless of framing, and prefer critiques of the form "the model supports a bolder claim than the text makes." Do not recommend hedging, appendix placement, or shorter length unless asked. See [[astra-ccc-proposal]].
