\section{Parameter vectors}\label{sec:B_parameters}

This appendix lists every parameter vector that the paper and this guide use. A value printed from the paper's
registry appears as in the paper; an exact fraction is an input. The last column points to the place in the guide
that uses the vector. The paper declares calibration B in its Table~A.5 and records every margin at B in its
Table~A.6 \paper{App.~A.9}.

\paragraph{Many classes} \paper{Prop.~A.6}.

\item \emph{Four classes.} The slot, costs, reserve, \(r_0\) and \(r_1\) of B; equal priors \(\reg{four_prior}\); point
values \(\reg{four_v1}\) and \(\reg{four_v2}\) (low group), \(\reg{four_v3}\) and \(\reg{four_v4}\) (high group).

\(G_2=(1-w)\delta_1+w\delta_{10}\), \(G_3=\delta_{10}\) with \(w=(1+2b_0+\lambda)/2=\reg{p2_many_weight}\);
\(R\sim U[0,8]\), \(p=1\), \(\rho=1/4\), \(c_H=10\), \(k=1/8\).
\end{itemize}
