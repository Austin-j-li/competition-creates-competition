This guide explains the order-slot version of the paper by Austin Li. It gives each result in two passes:
\begin{enumerate}[nosep,leftmargin=1.6em]
\item Part~I gives the intuition in words and the derivation, with every algebra step written out. It uses
no benchmark numbers;
\item Part~II puts the benchmark numbers on these results and reads the graphs of the strength path.
\end{enumerate}
\smallskip
Blue boxes give intuition. Orange boxes give notes for the panel. Green boxes state results. Red boxes
mark common errors. A tag such as \paper{(6)} or \paper{Prop.~2} gives the equation or the result in the
paper. The scripts in \texttt{verify/} recompute the numerical checks from the primitives. They use exact fractions or high-precision arithmetic.
Run them to reproduce the displayed values.
Decimal displays are rounded. Use the formulas and unrounded inputs for strict inequalities.
Section~\ref{sec:13_panel} prepares you for the panel. Appendix~\ref{sec:A_verification} explains how the numbers
were checked, Appendix~\ref{sec:B_parameters} lists the parameter vectors, and Appendix~\ref{sec:C_equation_map}
maps the paper to this guide.
