The order-slot model: an EGJ-like trading stage

One order of unit size arrives before the challenger decides. It comes from the informed investor or from a liquidity trader with an order of the same size. The market cannot tell which. Only the trading stage changes. The quality classes, their prior, the value distributions, the costs and the auction stay as in the paper. With costly research, as in Dow and Gorton (1997), entry also rises strictly across the paper's three intermediate strengths.

10What it keeps, and what it costs

Keeps

  • The paper's class model: prior and value distributions .
  • All of Proposition 2, with unique outcomes.
  • The paper's no-trade boundary .
  • Coexistence on in the benchmark.
  • With costly research, entry that rises strictly at 1.55, 1.60 and 1.65.
  • Every extension of the paper in substance (Section 9).
  • The access-to-prices result.
  • No off-path beliefs, and short proofs.
  • A buy-only version with the same results.

Costs

  • Unit orders. With , a 0.99 buy reaches an unused order. Under a prior belief there it earns 0.0964 against 0.0446 at , and part (iii) fails. A continuous menu needs EGJ's belief convention.
  • An assumed liquidity motive. A free uninformed trader would not trade. A holder hit by forced liquidity shocks gives the same trader, though not as the unique outcome.
  • No partial sale at 1.55–1.65. A mixed sale by class L appears only on . If the investor can choose when to trade, a delayed sale appears, but in one of two equilibria only.
  • Entry moves in steps. With a fixed , entry is flat at 1.55–1.65, at fixed information, and at the paper's exclusion reserve. Costly research restores the first of these.

The trade-off behind the step costs. With unit orders at the benchmark, the return to at caps the top posterior at . An EGJ-type branch with entry that rises at 1.55–1.65 needs a top posterior above 0.755. Tuned noise does both only in boxes about 0.001 wide. analytical Costly research avoids the trade-off: the cap fixes the top posterior at the strong strengths, while research below the cap moves continuously at the intermediate ones.
