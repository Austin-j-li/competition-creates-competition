# Role brief, phase 1: ideas that start from theory (Opus B)

Read `CORE_BRIEF.md` in this folder, then `../phase0/DOSSIER.md`.

## Your angle

Generate job-market paper candidates that start from an economic mechanism in M&A. Aim at a mechanism that a top-3 finance editor has not seen. It should change how people think about sale processes, bidder entry or information before deals.

- CCC is one seed. View it from a higher level. Ask what general principle it is a case of, and whether a different paper states that principle better. Most of your ideas should not be CCC extensions.
- Ask what the sale-process ledgers can reveal that a theory predicts and nobody measured. Examples: the order of contacts, NDAs, standstills, informal rounds, go-shops, the timing of rival entry against news and prices.
- Where it is natural, give each idea a structural hook: which primitive could be estimated, and which counterfactual it answers.
- Consider strategic information design by the seller, the target board or the bankers. Consider dynamics and timing, private equity against strategic bidders, leaks and trading before announcements, and the interaction of the stock market with the sale process.
