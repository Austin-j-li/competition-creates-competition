## Your task

Show what kind of M&A and corporate-finance paper wins on the job market and in top-3 finance journals. The other agents generate ideas. Your scan calibrates how the team ranks them.

1. **Job-market papers, 2021 to 2026.** Find 15 to 25 job-market papers in M&A or closely related corporate finance (sale processes, bidding, private equity buyouts, deal terms, information before deals, structural corporate finance). Prefer candidates who placed at strong business schools worldwide. For each paper, give the author, the PhD school, the placement if known, the title, the question, the method (theory, reduced form, structural, new data) and the data. Use the web: job-market candidate pages, school placement pages, SSRN and personal websites. Mark any fact you cannot verify as "unverified". Placements are the highest risk of error: prefer fewer verified entries over many guessed ones.
2. **Top-3 finance M&A papers, 2018 to 2026.** List 20 to 30 papers in JF, RFS and JFE on M&A sale processes, bidder entry, takeover auctions, structural M&A models and trading before deals. For each, give the question, the method and the data source.
3. **Patterns.** What do the winners share? Consider these features:
   - a new dataset;
   - a new fact;
   - a model that explains the fact;
   - a structural counterfactual;
   - a policy hook such as antitrust, disclosure or insider trading.
   How often is a hand-built dataset from filings the core? Which questions look saturated? Which look open?
4. **Gaps.** From the patterns, list 5 to 10 open questions in M&A that a candidate with Austin's assets could take. Score them with the rubric in the core brief. Give only one line each. You do not need full pitches.
