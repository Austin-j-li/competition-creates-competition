# Notation and consistency audit (talk preparation)

Method. The econ-skills `notation-clean` skill was run in audit mode with auction and market-microstructure conventions replacing its macroeconomic reserved-symbol table; `audit-econ` was run with its Math/Model template restricted to consistency (symbols, formulas, result statements and qualifiers, assumptions, numbers against `numerics/quantity_registry.csv`, cross-references, terminology). Two notation auditors and three consistency auditors worked independently on `paper/main.md` and `paper/online_appendix.md`; their findings were merged with agreement counts and each merged finding was re-checked by a verifier instructed to refute it. No paper, numerics, figure or table file was changed; the slides use the consistent versions below, and `talk/paper-sync.md` lists what the paper must adopt later.

Result: 57 merged findings; 20 confirmed as stated, 37 confirmed with a revised fix or severity, 0 refuted. Final severities: Critical 0, High 2, Mid 12, Low 42, None 1.
