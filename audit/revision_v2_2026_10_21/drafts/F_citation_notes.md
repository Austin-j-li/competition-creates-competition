## AcharyaJohnson2010

- **Status:** VERIFIED. The content check covers the published abstract and introduction.
- **Record:** Viral V. Acharya and Timothy C. Johnson (2010), “More Insiders, More Insider Trading: Evidence from Private-Equity Buyouts.” *Journal of Financial Economics* 98 (3): 500–523. DOI: `10.1016/j.jfineco.2010.08.002`.
- **Sources:** [Published abstract and introduction](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0304405X10001789); [DOI record](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1016/j.jfineco.2010.08.002).
