\textbf{Inputs.} Fix the benchmark strong strength, \(b\), and \(\rho\).
Use full orders.
Take \texttt{401} equally spaced points for the normalized threshold distance
\(d_\tau=(M-\tau)/(M-1/2)\) on \([0,1]\).
Add the benchmark \(\tau\), \(M-10^{-4}\), \(M-10^{-6}\), and \(M+10^{-6}\).
At the endpoints, use the stated mathematical limits and the tie convention, not unstable logarithms.
Compare Laplace and logistic laws at the same scale parameter.
Do not claim equal variance.
At a common scale, the Laplace experiment Blackwell-dominates the logistic experiment.
Do not infer an order of entry probabilities from this dominance.
