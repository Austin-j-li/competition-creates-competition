\subsection{C.6b. Retired exercise: price pooling at fixed orders}\label{oa-c-price-pools}

This exercise is retired. It was the regression for a one-parameter family of
continuations in which one order profile supported many pooling regions at a
high reserve. That family needed a positive low preparation cost.
At its pooled price, gross profit \(3\bar\mu_c\) fell below that cost, so the
buyer stayed out at the pooled price and prepared at each separate positive price.
Under the declared cost law the low cost is zero and \(3\bar\mu_c>0\).
The buyer at the pooled price then prepares. The prescribed preparation policy
fails its first buyer check, and no member of the family is a continuation.
A.11 states the argument.

Nothing replaces the exercise. Continuation identity, atom validation from the
full preimage, and the ban on an orders-only deduplication key all stay in force
through C.0, C.2, and C.6, where the live exercises use them.
The registry keys \texttt{pool\_*} that this exercise fed are withdrawn with it.
A run that still produces them reads a stale contract.
