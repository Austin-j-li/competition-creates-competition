The informed investor is an outside specialist with no net position in
the target. Its information is the challenger's quality class. It forms
that view from research on the target side of the match, not from news
on whether a bid arrives. Target shareholders gain when the price
recruits entry, because a holder through the sale receives sale
proceeds, not the interim price. Laplace noise is the extreme member
of a class of noise laws in which no order flow is conclusive;
\hyperref[result-proposition-a-5]{Proposition A.5} states the result
for the class.
