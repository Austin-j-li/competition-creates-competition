\textbf{Input declaration: additional noise laws in C.9.}

C.9 uses the benchmark vector and atomic costs. Its additional noise
laws have score bound \(1/b\). The hyperbolic secant density is
proportional to \(\operatorname{sech}(z/b)\). The Student law has
\(\nu=3\) degrees of freedom and scale \(b(\nu+1)/(2\sqrt{\nu})\). The
scale matches the score bound, not the variance. The prescribed orders
are \((1,-1)\).
