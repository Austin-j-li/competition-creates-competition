## 2. A.13: the general win gap

Because preparation depends on the observed price and independent cost, conditioning on preparation leaves the class posterior at \(\mu(x)\). For any continuous \(F\), define
\[
W_j(F)=\mathbb E_j\!\left[F(\theta)\mathbf1\{\theta\ge p\}\right].
\]
Then
\[
w_x=\Pr(W\mid X=x,A)
=\mu(x)W_H(F)+[1-\mu(x)]W_L(F),
\qquad
\Pr(W\mid X=x)=e(x)w_x.
\]
Under full orders with fixed slot and prior,
\[
w_1-w_{-1}
=[M(\pi)-m(\pi)]\,\bigl[W_H(F)-W_L(F)\bigr].
\]
At prior \(1/2\), \(M-m=\vartheta\). With the standing all-admissible class ranking and continuous \(F\), integration by parts gives
\[
W_H(F)-W_L(F)=\int_{[p,\infty)}D(y)\,dF(y)
=\mathbb E_F[D(R)]\ge0.
\]
These replace \(1\), \(\ell/r\), and \(1-\ell/r\) in A.13. [Model:225–228](/Users/austinli/Projects/competition-creates-competition/overleaf/slot/sections/02_model.tex:225) [A8:179–191](/Users/austinli/Projects/competition-creates-competition/overleaf/slot/appendix/A8_empirical.tex:179)

For a fixed slot, the gap **rises strictly** from \(F_0\) to a stronger \(F_1\) exactly when
\[
\int D(y)\,dF_1(y)>\int D(y)\,dF_0(y).
\]
A sufficient family-wide condition is that \(D\) be nondecreasing over the incumbent values moved by the strengthening, with a strict move across a region where \(D\) rises. Class ranking supplies \(D\ge0\), not this monotonicity. When entry has returned to \(\rho\), the prior-\(1/2\) covariance in A.13 becomes
\[
\operatorname{Cov}(X,\mathbf1_W)
=(b+\lambda/2)\rho\vartheta\,[W_H(F)-W_L(F)].
\]
It is strictly positive exactly when the bracket is positive. [A8:173–175](/Users/austinli/Projects/competition-creates-competition/overleaf/slot/appendix/A8_empirical.tex:173) [A8:185–191](/Users/austinli/Projects/competition-creates-competition/overleaf/slot/appendix/A8_empirical.tex:185)

A counterexample stays inside the ranked-class, continuous-FOSD model. Set \(p=1/2\), \(G_L=\delta_1\), \(G_H=\delta_2\), and \(F_r=U[0,r]\) for \(r>2\). Then
\[
W_H(F_r)=2/r,\quad W_L(F_r)=1/r,\quad
w_1-w_{-1}=[M-m]/r,
\]
which **falls** as the incumbent strengthens. Indeed, \(\Delta_T(F_r)=1-3/(2r)\) rises over the same family. Full orders can hold throughout: \(\pi=\rho=\lambda=\beta=1/3\) would make the prior differ from the displayed covariance case; taking instead \(\pi=1/2,\ \rho=1/2,\ \lambda=\beta=1/3,\ k=1/100,\ c_H=2\) gives \(m=2/7\), \(\Delta_T>1/4\), and \(\rho m\Delta_T>1/28>k\), while high-cost preparation is excluded. Thus the trading bound forces full orders and the falling win gap is an equilibrium comparison. The counterexample uses the paper’s general class ranking and slot restrictions. [Model:57–66](/Users/austinli/Projects/competition-creates-competition/overleaf/slot/sections/02_model.tex:57) [Model:118–125](/Users/austinli/Projects/competition-creates-competition/overleaf/slot/sections/02_model.tex:118) [A2:219–225](/Users/austinli/Projects/competition-creates-competition/overleaf/slot/appendix/A2_beliefs_trading.tex:219) The benchmark’s rising formula follows instead from its extra restriction \(r<h\), which keeps \(W_H=1\). ## 3. Auction objects outside \((\Delta_T,\tau,t_0,t_L)\)

- **Class profit levels and \(B_F\).** Proposition 1 defines
  \[
  g_j(F)=\int_p^\infty[1-G_j(y)]F(y)\,dy,\qquad
  B_F(\mu)=(1-\mu)g_L(F)+\mu g_H(F).
  \]
  These are used for the fixed-information crossing probability, cost-support inequalities, the A.5 construction, multi-class group entry, private-signal entry, the welfare gain, and the empirical ceiling and announcement positivity. A threshold \(\tau\) alone does not give these profit **levels** or the size of a surplus gain. - **Reserve admissibility, payoff kernels, and class tails.** A.1 uses \(D(p^-)\), \(D(y)\), \(pF(p)\), the fixed-\(R\) kernel \(\xi(R)\), and \(t(\theta),g(\theta)\). Their general replacements are exactly [A1:123–142](/Users/austinli/Projects/competition-creates-competition/overleaf/slot/appendix/A1_payoffs.tex:123) and [A1:170–189](/Users/austinli/Projects/competition-creates-competition/overleaf/slot/appendix/A1_payoffs.tex:170); in particular

## 4. Wrong or overclaimed scope

1. **“Every result holds for every order profile” is too broad.** The belief lemma does; the price lemma assumes E1–E4 and E6, and the trading lemma assumes E1, E4, E6 and an entry floor. Amend the A.2 introduction accordingly. [A2:7–8](/Users/austinli/Projects/competition-creates-competition/overleaf/slot/appendix/A2_beliefs_trading.tex:7) [A2:110–111](/Users/austinli/Projects/competition-creates-competition/overleaf/slot/appendix/A2_beliefs_trading.tex:110) [A2:173–175](/Users/austinli/Projects/competition-creates-competition/overleaf/slot/appendix/A2_beliefs_trading.tex:173)

2. **A.5 is not a theorem for an arbitrary FOSD family.** Its assertion is correctly written for uniform incumbents, but the main-text “every pair of ranked classes and every prior” must continue to be read with its immediately following uniform-incumbent restriction. The zero-spread endpoint and \(g_j(r)\to0\) are construction steps, not consequences of FOSD ordering alone. [§6:25–36](/Users/austinli/Projects/competition-creates-competition/overleaf/slot/sections/06_robustness.tex:25) [A6:157–189](/Users/austinli/Projects/competition-creates-competition/overleaf/slot/appendix/A6_robustness.tex:157)

3. **The A.11 impact-asymmetry test needs prior \(1/2\).** At general prior, even with no recruitment,
   \[
   I_+-I_-=\rho\Delta_T\{[M(\pi)-\pi]-[\pi-m(\pi)]\}.
   \]
   Recruitment adds \((1-\rho)A_F(M)\). Thus “\(I_+>I_-\) iff a purchase recruits” is not a general two-class implication. The main-text test is stated inside the benchmark scope; preserve that qualification in any general theorem. [A8:63–67](/Users/austinli/Projects/competition-creates-competition/overleaf/slot/appendix/A8_empirical.tex:63) [§8:3–5](/Users/austinli/Projects/competition-creates-competition/overleaf/slot/sections/08_empirical.tex:3) [§8:19–26](/Users/austinli/Projects/competition-creates-competition/overleaf/slot/sections/08_empirical.tex:19)

Its reserve-failure component \(pF(p)\) can fall under strengthening. State monotonicity of \(A_F(M)\) as a separate family condition. For a known prior away from \(1/2\), counts themselves identify \(\lambda\); the A.12 nonidentification statement does not carry over.
