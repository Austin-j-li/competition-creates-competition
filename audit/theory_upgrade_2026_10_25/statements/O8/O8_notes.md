# O8. Zero research at the sale boundary

## Result

Proved. At prior one half and tau above one half, zero research exists exactly when rho Delta_T is at most 2k. At equality it has one research, order-flow, belief, price, entry and ownership outcome, with a family of hypothetical-success order strategies. Class H uses any mixture of buying and abstention; class L uses any mixture of selling and abstention. The mixtures are independent choices of the two class-contingent strategies. Neither is realized at zero research.

An indifferent investor may mix; either challenger tie rule gives the same result because the challenger strictly abstains at the prior. When an indifferent investor abstains, its rule selects abstention at equality. The scope also includes ranked classes, continuous incumbent law, positive spread and profit gap, positive trading and research costs, liquidity share strictly between zero and one half, and cap strictly between zero and one.

## Argument

At conjectured intensity zero, each class has the same liquidity order law. Every order and every price therefore carries the prior, and the high-cost challenger strictly abstains. Competitive pricing gives one common price. At these fixed prices, both correct orders earn rho Delta_T/2 minus k, abstention earns zero, and wrong-signed orders lose strictly.

The best success value is the positive part of rho Delta_T/2 minus k. A joint deviation gives intensity times that value minus the quadratic research cost. At the boundary the value is zero, and the unique optimal research choice is zero. Correct orders and abstention are tied, so all stated mixtures obey order optimality. Above the boundary the success value is positive and zero research is not optimal.

The fragment also gives the exhaustion argument for positive research. A strict full purchase is necessary. With full recruitment the sale lemma and the recruiting equation determine the outcome; at the entry floor the purchase payoff forces a strict full sale, and the floor equation determines the outcome. At rho Delta_T = 2k, positive intensity makes the floor purchase lose, so floor-entry research has no positive-intensity endpoint at that boundary.

For positive floor research, evaluating the recruiting residual at its root gives (1-rho) Delta_T m_lambda_Q, strictly positive. Hence the floor root lies below the recruiting root. Their clipped posteriors give exactly the coexistence band; the band is empty when both roots reach the cap.

## Planned claim and consequences

The planned outcome classification holds. A statement that the zero-research boundary has a unique complete strategy tuple is false. The precise statement is uniqueness of the zero-research outcome with the strategy family above. This is the boundary qualification in Lemma A.16, label `lem:a5-outcomes`, part (a).

Corollary 3 includes zero research at r_N, with these mixtures, and excludes positive floor-entry research there. The list of outcomes therefore has closed [r_R,r_N] and open (r_N,r_Q) endpoints. The accepted mathematics in statements/O1/ agrees. Its notes, Section 6, describe the same distinction; the proof fragment is unchanged.
