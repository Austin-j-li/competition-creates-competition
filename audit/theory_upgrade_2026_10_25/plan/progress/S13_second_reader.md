## Verdict

No mathematical error or gap was found in any stated result or proof, and every main-text number recomputes. The paper is ready apart from three cheap edits (an abstract clause that claims more than its proposition, one self-contradictory sentence in Section 8, and three bibliography entries that print surnames only) and a set of exposition changes that would help a referee at a top journal: the introduction and Proposition 4(d) carry too many qualifiers in one breath, Section 4.2 is long for the stretch between Propositions 2 and 3, and the appendix is 108 pages.

The four propositions and three corollaries carry the story. Proposition 1 moves the class gap from profit to proceeds, Proposition 2 turns that move into entry, Proposition 3 says where the quiet and the informative market both stand, and Proposition 4 makes the information itself a choice; the corollaries show each on one path of strengths. The general-first order works: a reader meets no number before Section 4.2, and the example then does real work. The introduction sells the results accurately, and its first paragraph is bold; its later paragraphs are faithful transcriptions of the propositions and read as such.

## 1. Mathematics

No error or gap. The checks that a referee would run, and their outcome:

- Proposition 2 (A.3): the takeover-share condition (6) follows from the ratio inequality in Step 4; the outbidding case gives a strict rise exactly when the low class can beat the weak incumbent; uniqueness at every strength uses only the nonzero-order bounds, so the statement's range \(0<\beta<1/2\) is right even though the model restricts \(\beta\le1/3\). The decisive-advantage lemma's cost floor \((h^2-p^2)/(2h)=399/80\) is the supremum of \(g_H\) over \(r>h\), as claimed.
- Corollary 1 (A.3): the band \(82/59<u<2686/1559\) is exactly \(\tau(r_1)<M<\tau(r_2)\); the right side of (H2) is implied inside the band; the prior range at \(1+u=\mathrm e\) is the intersection of the three ranges, as stated.
- Proposition 3 (A.4): Step 1 of the proof of part (a) correctly disposes of \(\pi\ge\tau\), so the "if and only if" holds without a side condition on the quiet market's entry; the convexity and the bound \(\pi<1/(1+\rho)\) follow from the two spread bounds; part (b)'s up-set and down-set argument needs no sign on \(\tau\); part (c)(i) uses the balance condition exactly once and the monotonicity of \(\gamma\) once; the uniform case (c)(iii) is right (the key inequality is \(r\Delta_T'\ge\Delta_T\), which holds because every low-class value lies in \([0,r]\)); part (d) constructs an equilibrium at every strength, including \(\tau\ge1\).
- Corollary 2 and Corollary A.2 (A.4): the five forms exhaust the high-class pairs, the order of the marked strengths follows from the lemma on the example path, and the priors \(\pi_A^{\rm lo}<\pi_A^{\rm hi}=\tau(r_A)<\pi_{\max}=\tau(r_*)\) divide the cases as stated. The two-interval path of the paragraph after the proof of part (d) has coexistence exactly on \([0,0.482764]\cup[1.150106,2]\), as the text says.
- Proposition 4 (A.5): the research equation, the uniqueness of its root for \(\Delta_T>2k\), the slopes and the kink at \(\lambda_R=(1-\rho)k/(2a\rho)\), the cap spread, the ownership ratio and the interval result of part (d) are all correct; the proof of (d) handles the plateau \(\tau=M_{\bar\lambda}\) and the case in which the path ends before the ceiling.
- Corollary 3 and the floor lemma (A.5): the three outcomes exhaust the equilibria under the default tie rules; the floor margin's piecewise derivative bound is a valid lower bound on each piece; the decomposition of \(\varphi_\rho\) on \([r_U,r_C]\) gives its positivity there, so \(r_Q\) is the unique zero; Step 6 shows that every equilibrium above \(r_N\) is one of the two named ones.
- Appendices A.6 to A.8: the control proposition, the cost-law proposition, the uniform-family construction, the signal proposition, the welfare formulas (both gains at \(r_1\) and both changes at \(c_H=12/5\) recompute), the payment-rule spread, the reserve kernel, the weighted test, the identification formulas, the covariance formulas and the announcement mean all check.
