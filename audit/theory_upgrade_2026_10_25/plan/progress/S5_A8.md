## 6. Issues for the coordinator

1. **Corollary 2 label.** Parts (e) and (h) cite `cor:example-coexistence` (from `statements/B.tex`), with Table A.2 beside it in the proofs for the forms on each interval and the count "exactly two equilibria", which the frozen Corollary 2 does not state. The coexistence writer must define that label.
2. **A.10(vi)(d), last sentence, under the abstaining investor rule.** "When an indifferent high-cost challenger may prepare with any probability, entry after a purchase at r_C can take any value in [ρ, 1]" names no investor rule. At e(1) = ρ the purchase earns ρΔ_T(r_C)(1 − M) − k, which the hypotheses bound only by its right limit, a nonnegative number. The proof covers the mixing investor at every e(1), and every e(1) in (ρ, 1] under both investor rules; under the abstaining rule the endpoint e(1) = ρ fails at the knife edge ρΔ_T(r_C)(1 − M) = k. The proof says exactly this much, and the statement is left as frozen. Proposed fix, one clause: "When an indifferent investor may mix over its best orders and an indifferent high-cost challenger may prepare with any probability, ...". The example is unaffected: its investor mixes, and ρΔ_T(r_C)m > k because r_C > r_S.
3. **Γ in A.12(vi).** A1 still calls the kernel ξ; S8 renames it Γ. The proof defines Γ(x) = ∫_p^x D(y)dy itself and points to Appendix A.1, so it reads correctly before and after S8.
4. **Lemma numbers.** The D notes assumed `lem:a4-order` prints A.5; with the foundation lemmas of A2 it prints A.8. Nothing in A8 hard-codes a number.
5. **Dependencies on other packages.** The proofs cite `lem:twonumbers` (A2, the foundation writer's), `prop:region` printing "Corollary 1" (A3), `tab:a4-set` and `lem:a4-order` (A4). A rename of any of these in a later stage needs the same rename here.
