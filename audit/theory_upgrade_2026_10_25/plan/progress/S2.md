## Language scan

Usage: `python3 audit/slot_manuscript_2026_10_23/scripts/_coordinator/language_scan.py [--doc DIR] [--extra PATH ...] [--pdf FILE ...] [--no-figcode] [--no-doc]`. Exit status 0 when the scan ran to the end, 1 when it stopped or could not read a source, 2 for a usage error. A stopped scan ends with the line `scan INCOMPLETE: ...`.

Default scope: `main.tex`, `sections/*.tex`, `appendix/*.tex`, `comparison.tex`, `slides.tex`, and the string literals of the figure program. Comments, `comment` environments and `\iffalse` blocks are left out. The arguments of `\reg`, `\label`, `\ref`, `\cite`, `\input` and `\includegraphics` are blanked. A phrase broken over two lines or tied with `~` is found. `--pdf` scans the text of built PDFs page by page, with a second pass that joins hyphenated line ends.

Categories: status (rule 1), defensive (rule 2), history and process (rule 3), example naming (rule 4), names (tools, agents, models, hosts, accounts and home paths, written in pieces inside the program), house, interval claim, placeholder, and allowed (the two tie-rule phrases, printed and kept out of the total).

A synthetic file of planted violations and planted clean cases was scanned in a scratch folder. Every planted violation was found, and no planted clean case was reported.
