## Mathematical evidence for the prose repairs

**Research posterior gap.** Take point classes at ten and one, reserve one half, high cost three, zero-cost probability one quarter, trading cost one fiftieth, liquidity probability one quarter, research cost coefficient sixty-one fifteenths and cap nine tenths. Let the incumbent be uniform on a short interval of half-width one hundredth centered at the strength. The whole incumbent support lies between the class values. The spread equals strength minus the low-class value, and high-class profit equals the high-class value minus strength. The capped research equation of Proposition 4 gives the following two recruiting economies, both strictly below the cap and with strictly profitable sales:

| Strength | Spread | Research | Purchase posterior | Preparation threshold | Posterior minus threshold |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| five | four | 0.2000000000 | 0.6666666667 | 0.6000000000 | 0.0666666667 |
| five and one tenth | four and one tenth | 0.2035667977 | 0.6691362690 | 0.6122448980 | 0.0568913711 |

The spread, research and purchase posterior rise while its distance above the threshold falls. The repair retains the three increasing quantities and leaves the moving threshold to the recruiting condition.

**Research endpoint.** Restrict the example path to a closed interval that reaches the cap strength and stops strictly between the cap strength and the entry ceiling. All hypotheses of Proposition 4(d) persist, but the recruiting set ends at the last strength of that restricted path with a strictly positive posterior margin. The last-point formulation in the repair covers this case and a threshold plateau.

**Cheap research.** The exact margin has derivative \([1+\rho\mathbf 1\{\rho J>k\}]J'-2a\lambda_E'(\tau)\tau'\) almost everywhere. A small positive cost cannot make it increase on a path with decreasing \(J\). The appendix names the slope comparison, rather than small cost alone, as the condition.

**Preparation reports.** Let both classes put all their mass at the reserve and let the continuous incumbent law be uniform between twice and three times the reserve. Every prepared challenger loses, sale proceeds equal the reserve with or without preparation, gross challenger profit is zero, the investor abstains, and entry equals \(\rho\). Preparation remains uncertain, but every preparation-report return is zero. These primitives satisfy the model's weak class ranking. The positive-spread setting of A8 rules them out.
