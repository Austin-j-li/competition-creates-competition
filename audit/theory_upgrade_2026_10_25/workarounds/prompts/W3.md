# W3: where the research branch ends

Weakness key: `W3-research-branch-end`

Where: overleaf/slot/appendix/A5_research.tex, "The branch at calibration B" (around lines 286 to 314) and the research lemmas (`lem:a5-middle` line 122, `lem:a5-strong` line 203); overleaf/slot/sections/05_research.tex lines 119 to 126 (the branch [r_R, r̄_R)).

What is general. Branch R (class H buys after a successful research draw, class L abstains, only a purchase recruits) exists where 1/2 < τ < 1, φ_a = J − k − 2aλ_E(τ) ≥ 0, λ_R < λ̄ and ρΔ ≤ 2k. The research intensity on the branch is λ_R = Λ(Δ), strictly increasing in Δ, with dΛ/dΔ = m/(2a − Δm′) > 0. Every branch point has J > k.

The weakness. The benchmark statement "the branch is an interval [r_R, r̄_R) that ends where research reaches the cap" needs (i) single crossing of φ_a and (ii) ρ(2aλ̄ + k) ≤ 2k·m_λ̄: the cap is reached before the low class starts selling at ρΔ = 2k. Counterexample to (ii) (Astra): h = 10, ℓ = 1, p = 0.5, c_H = 4.5, k = 0.5, ρ = 0.5, β = 0.2, a = 0.01, λ̄ = 0.4 gives r_N = 5.83 < r_cap = 7.28, so the branch ends where the low class starts selling, before research reaches the cap.

Open research question, the main one here: what happens beyond ρΔ = 2k? Is there a continuation branch (class L sells too after research, research optimality 2aλ = ½[U_H(1) + U_L(−1)] or the right analogue) on which research and entry keep rising, so that "research rises with strength" survives on a connected set of equilibria from r_R to the cap? Construct it, derive its research intensity and entry, and check existence and monotonicity. Also: is single crossing of φ_a implied by something natural (for example J increasing, or a bound on a)?
