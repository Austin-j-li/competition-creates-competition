# W4: orders predict the winner

Weakness key: `W4-winner-gap`

Where: Proposition A.13 (`prop:winner`, overleaf/slot/appendix/A8_empirical.tex lines 166 to 196) and its summary in overleaf/slot/sections/08_empirical.tex.

What is general. In the benchmark, the probability that the challenger wins after order x, given entry, is w_x = μ(x) + (1 − μ(x))ℓ/r, and the purchase-minus-sale gap rises with r. In general, w_x = μ(x)W_H(F) + (1 − μ(x))W_L(F); under full two-sided trade, w_1 − w_{−1} = (M − m)(W_H − W_L), and W_H − W_L = E_F[D(R)] ≥ 0.

The weakness. The gap can fall with strength. Example: G_L = δ_1, G_H = δ_2, p = 1/2, R ~ U[0, r], r > 2: the gap is (M − m)/r, which falls in r. The post-ceiling covariance between orders and winning also needs W_H > W_L.

Directions: a condition on where class values are dense relative to the incumbent support; an exact condition along the uniform family; a reformulation that is general (for example a statement about the level of the gap, its sign, or a ratio such as the winner odds ratio, rather than its trend). Also judge whether the trend in the gap is economically central or whether the sign is what an empiricist would test.
