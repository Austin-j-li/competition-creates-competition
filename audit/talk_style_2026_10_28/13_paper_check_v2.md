The paper calls Dow, Goldstein and Guembel the closest antecedent. The table lists both that paper and Edmans, Goldstein and Jiang; its bold emphasis does not change the stated economic result.
- **Slides 4–8 and B8:** The alternative arrivals, order menu, information sequence, auction payoffs, class means, price equation, and investor payoff match §2–§3. The six conditions and both Bayes formulas match Definition A.1. Order beliefs remain separate from beliefs at a price. Proposition 1 uses the correct weak signs and class-gap identity.

The unique outcomes, class entry, total entry, ownership inequalities, and four tie-rule pairs also match. B1's wider domain \(0<\beta<1/2\) is valid for Proposition 2. It does not extend the all-profile zero-order bound beyond \(\beta\le1/3\).
