# 30-minute talk — outline for sign-off (28 October 2026)

Decisions in force:
- 30 minutes of speech; 15 minutes for questions.
- Players in plain words: first buyer, second buyer, informed investor. Also: seller, liquidity trader,
  market makers; good fit, poor fit; diligence bill; prepare (action), entry (its chance); stock price,
  informative, uninformative; shareholder spread; buyer profit; belief, top belief, threshold.
- Literature early, one minute, with Edmans, Goldstein and Jiang (2015) as the anchor: the stock price as an
  information source for a real decision.
- Model slides carry a strip: Players · Timing · Information · Actions · Payoffs · Equilibrium.
- Result slides have three parts: statement in a theorem box, one line of intuition, one picture or table.
- Conditions in words on the slide face; exact forms on hidden backups.
- Notation on the main slides: F, π, k, μ, Δ_T, m and M, τ (λ returns on the research slide with a reminder).
- No example number before slide 12. One metaphor: one value, two pockets.
