Competition creates competition

Stock prices and the discovery of takeover bidders

Austin Li · UCL School of Management

Read the paper PDF Online appendix PDF Seminar talk slides

When can a stronger bidder attract a rival?

A stronger incumbent makes an acquisition less profitable for a challenger. It can also make information more valuable to investors, so their trades reveal more through the target's stock price.

Under the paper's conditions, that information prompts a challenger to prepare when it would otherwise stay out.
