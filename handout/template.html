Competition creates competition

Stock prices and the discovery of takeover bidders

Austin Li · Department of Economics, University College London

When can a stronger bidder draw a second buyer into the sale?

A stronger incumbent leaves a challenger less to gain from preparing a bid. It also makes what target shareholders receive depend more on the challenger's value, so an investor who knows that value has more reason to trade on it.

The stock price carries that information to the challenger before it commits. Under the conditions of Proposition 2, a stronger competitor can then bring in a buyer that would otherwise stay out: competition creates competition.
