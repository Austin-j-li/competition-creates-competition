Competition creates competition

Stock prices and the discovery of takeover bidders

Austin Li · Department of Economics, University College London

When can a stronger bidder draw a rival bidder into the sale?

A stronger incumbent bidder leaves a potential rival bidder less to gain from preparing a bid. The same auction makes what target shareholders receive depend more on the rival's quality, so an informed trader who knows that quality has more reason to trade on it.

One order in the target's stock can carry that knowledge to the rival before it commits. In Proposition 2, a stronger incumbent then brings in a high-cost rival that a weaker incumbent could not attract: competition creates competition.
