Competition creates competition

Stock prices and the discovery of takeover bidders

Austin Li · Department of Economics, University College London

Research brief Read the paper Seminar talk

Contents

When can a stronger bidder draw a second buyer into the sale?

A stronger incumbent leaves a challenger less to gain from preparing a bid. The same auction makes what target shareholders receive depend more on the challenger's quality, so an investor who knows that quality has more reason to trade on it.

One order in the target's stock can carry that knowledge to the challenger before it commits. In Proposition 2, a stronger incumbent then brings in a high-cost buyer that a weaker incumbent could not attract: competition creates competition.
