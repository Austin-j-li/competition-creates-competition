Conclusion and next steps

The bidder pool in a takeover depends on the information available before buyers commit. A stronger incumbent can raise the sensitivity of target proceeds to challenger quality, make informed trading profitable, and bring in a challenger that a weaker incumbent would not have faced. Sale terms therefore reach participation through two channels, the buyer's expected payment and the information the market supplies, and the two can pull in opposite directions.

Theory

The next step is the seller's choice of terms with the full equilibrium correspondence retained; multiplicity is what stops me from identifying the seller's objective with an envelope of found revenues. A related question is whether committing to terms before trading does better than revising them after observing prices. Alternative formats would show how much of the mechanism is specific to the second-price payment rule. Endogenous investor research would add manipulation through the real decision that responds to the price (Goldstein and Guembel 2008).

Empirics

The empirical counterpart of the model's decision is the start of substantive diligence or a proposal that requires costly preparation, not the count of public offers. Online Appendix D specifies an institutional pilot that reconstructs, from merger-background narratives in proxy and tender-offer filings and from contemporaneous releases, the initial approach, public visibility, buyer contacts, data access, diligence entry, proposals, withdrawals, and final selection. Each event carries two dates, when it happened and when it became public, because a proxy can describe a private meeting the market knew nothing about at the time. The first output is a sourced chronology of eligible decision intervals, with exclusions and ambiguous cases kept in view.

None of this identifies a causal effect. A target price that rises before a competing bid can anticipate that bid, and a challenger that wins is not thereby shown to have entered on a favorable price. A causal design would need a shift in information transmission that leaves values, preparation costs, and financing alone, and no such instrument is asserted; the alternative is a structural exercise that models anticipated entry and learning together. No sample, estimated effect, or instrument is reported.
