SPEAKER NOTES:
Four stages, left to right, one per click. Before trade, the target commits to the sale rule. The incumbent is already prepared; its value is private, but its distribution F is public, and F is the only thing the results move. The rival’s synergy class, high or low, is drawn now; under the high class its values are higher. Then one order trades: an informed trader who knows the rival’s synergy class, or a liquidity trader. Market makers see the order but not who placed it, and set the price. The rival sees that price and its own cost, and prepares or stays out. A prepared rival learns its value and bids. Values and costs are per share; everyone is risk neutral. From here I go backward: each stage is paid through the later ones, so I start with the auction.
