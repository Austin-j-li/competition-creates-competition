SPEAKER NOTES:
Last stage first. Fix a prepared rival with value theta and read the picture from left to right as the incumbent’s value R rises. Below the reserve, the rival wins and pays p. Between p and theta, the rival still wins but pays R, so the price climbs. Above theta the incumbent wins and pays theta. The dark area is what shareholders are paid; the grey area is what the rival keeps. Averaging the two heights over R gives t of theta and g of theta, and they add up to theta. Two numbers carry forward: the shareholder spread, Delta T, and rival gross profit, B of mu. A stronger incumbent puts more weight on the right of the picture, where shareholders get more and the rival less.
