    | [16pt bold #1C1C1C]{Lemma A.1}[16pt #1C1C1C]{ (order probabilities and beliefs), for any order strategy: each order has positive probability in each synergy class. For a sale or a purchase, the chance of that order in class }$j$[16pt #1C1C1C]{ lies between }$b$[16pt #1C1C1C]{ and }$b+\lambda$[16pt #1C1C1C]{, where }$b=(1-\lambda )\beta$[16pt #1C1C1C]{ is the probability of a liquidity order in one direction and }$\lambda$[16pt #1C1C1C]{ is presence. With }$u=\lambda /b$[16pt #1C1C1C]{, each nonzero order has likelihood ratio between }$1/(1+u)$[16pt #1C1C1C]{ and }$1+u$[16pt #1C1C1C]{, so the belief }$\mu$[16pt #1C1C1C]{ after that order lies in }$[m(\pi ),M(\pi )]$[16pt #1C1C1C]{, where }$M(\pi )=\pi (1+u)/[\pi (1+u)+1-\pi ]$[16pt #1C1C1C]{ and }$m(\pi )=\pi /[\pi +(1-\pi )(1+u)]$[16pt #1C1C1C]{. The no-order case lies in the same band when }$\beta \le 1/3$[16pt #1C1C1C]{. Under full orders, high synergy buys and low synergy sells, the beliefs after a sale, no order, a purchase are }$(m(\pi ),\pi ,M(\pi ))$[16pt #1C1C1C]{. A belief at a price lies between the smallest and largest beliefs of the orders with that price.}

    | [16pt bold #1C1C1C]{Lemma A.2}[16pt #1C1C1C]{ (entry and prices): entry }$e(x)\ge \rho$[16pt #1C1C1C]{ after each order }$x$[16pt #1C1C1C]{, with entry floor }$\rho \in (0,1)$[16pt #1C1C1C]{. The price after order }$x$[16pt #1C1C1C]{ is }$P(x)=t_{0}+e(x)[t_{L}-t_{0}+\Delta_{T}\,\mu (x)]$[16pt #1C1C1C]{, with payoff }$t_{0}$[16pt #1C1C1C]{ without entry and }$t_{L}$[16pt #1C1C1C]{ under low synergy. When }$\Delta_{T}>0$[16pt #1C1C1C]{ and rival gross profit rises with the belief }$\mu$[16pt #1C1C1C]{, equal prices give equal beliefs, so the price reveals }$\mu (x)$[16pt #1C1C1C]{; entry is }$\rho$[16pt #1C1C1C]{ when }$\mu (x)<\tau$[16pt #1C1C1C]{ and }$1$[16pt #1C1C1C]{ when }$\mu (x)\ge \tau$[16pt #1C1C1C]{, with threshold }$\tau$[16pt #1C1C1C]{ (at }$\mu (x)=\tau$[16pt #1C1C1C]{, any entry in }$[\rho ,1]$[16pt #1C1C1C]{ can hold when an indifferent costly rival may prepare with any probability); prices rise strictly with }$\mu$[16pt #1C1C1C]{.}
