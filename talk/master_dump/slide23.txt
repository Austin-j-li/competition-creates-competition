    | [14pt bold #1C1C1C]{Lemma A.3}[14pt #1C1C1C]{ (informed trading): the gain before order cost }$k$[14pt #1C1C1C]{ from a correct purchase under high synergy is }$K_{H}=e(1)\,\Delta_{T}\,[1-\mu (1)]$[14pt #1C1C1C]{; from a correct sale under low synergy, }$K_{L}=e(-1)\,\Delta_{T}\,\mu (-1)$[14pt #1C1C1C]{. Under every order strategy, }$\rho \,\Delta_{T}[1-M(\pi )]\le K_{H}\le \Delta_{T}$[14pt #1C1C1C]{ and }$\rho \,\Delta_{T}\,m(\pi )\le K_{L}\le \Delta_{T}$[14pt #1C1C1C]{. With }$\kappa (\pi )=\mathrm{min}{m(\pi ),1-M(\pi )}$[14pt #1C1C1C]{, each gain lies between }$\rho \,\kappa (\pi )\,\Delta_{T}$[14pt #1C1C1C]{ and }$\Delta_{T}$[14pt #1C1C1C]{; the purchase bound uses }$1-M(\pi )$[14pt #1C1C1C]{ and the sale bound uses }$m(\pi )$[14pt #1C1C1C]{. A wrong-signed order earns at most }$-k$[14pt #1C1C1C]{.     | [14pt #1C1C1C]{Four-step proof: 1. Weak: each gain is at most }$\Delta_{T}$[14pt #1C1C1C]{, below }$k$[14pt #1C1C1C]{, so no informed order; the belief stays at the prior; entry stays at }$\rho$[14pt #1C1C1C]{. 2.
