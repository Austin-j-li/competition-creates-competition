SPEAKER NOTES:
One stage earlier: the rival. It sees the price, not the order, and it does not know its own synergy class: that is what diligence reveals. So the price is its only news, and it reads it by Bayes’ rule. It prepares when rival gross profit at that belief covers its cost. Two cases. With probability rho preparation is free, and since rival gross profit is never negative that rival always prepares: rho is the entry floor. Otherwise the cost is c H, and the rival prepares only when the price is good enough news. So entry takes two values: rho, or one. That is what a purchase can do: attract the costly rival.
