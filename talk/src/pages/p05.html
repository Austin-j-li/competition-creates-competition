A stronger incumbent can bring the rival in

Preview Two opposing effects of incumbent strength.

Through the share price

A stronger incumbent incentivises informed traders to trade, making the share price more informative.

In the auction

A stronger incumbent is harder to outbid and leaves less profit.

Main resultAs incumbent strength rises, rival entry can first rise through the share price, and then fall back once the incumbent is too strong to beat.
