Predictions · Paper §8 · Full two-sided trade, prior below \(\tau\)

Weight each price impact by how often its order occurs. Weighted purchase impact beats weighted sale impact exactly when a purchase recruits the rival.

  • At prior \(1/2\), order frequencies match. Compare raw impacts: \(P(1)-P(0)\) against \(P(0)-P(-1)\).
  • The price gap \(P(1)-P(-1)\) splits into a synergy part, \(\rho \Delta_{T}[M(\pi )-m(\pi )]\), and an entry part. The entry part is positive exactly under recruitment, and it sits wholly in the purchase impact.
  1. Entry can rise with incumbent strength only when the price lands before preparation starts. Hide the price, and entry can only fall.
  2. Past the entry ceiling, orders stop predicting entry. Orders keep predicting the winner when \(W_{H}>W_{L}\).
  3. At prior \(1/2\), order counts cannot separate slot types. Prices can.

Date the price before preparation starts. Record the zero order.

Test recruitment in prices first. The sharpest test needs no auction object.

In the example at \(r_{1}\), the entry part is % of the price gap. Informed-trader share and announcement detail: Backup B13, page 2.
