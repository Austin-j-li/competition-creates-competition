Players

Five risk-neutral players; all draws are mutually independent.

The takeover contest

Target

Listed firm for sale; standalone value \(0\); shareholders receive the auction payout \(V_{T}\).

Incumbent

Already prepared; knows its private value \(R\sim F\), with \(F\) public.

Rival

Synergy class \(j\in \{H,\,L\}\) (\(\mathrm{Pr}(H)=\pi\)), value \(\theta \sim G_{j}\) (\(G_{H}\) FOSD \(G_{L}\)). Diligence cost \(C\): \(0\) (prob. \(\rho\)) or \(c_{H}>0\) (prob. \(1-\rho\)).

The stock market

Traders

An informed trader (prob. \(\lambda\)) knows the rival’s class \(j\); a liquidity trader (prob. \(1-\lambda\)) trades for non‑informational reasons.

Market makers

Competitive dealers; see the stock order, not who sent it, and quote share price \(P\).
