Who the informed trader is

The informed trader is an outside investor with sector expertise. It holds no net position in the target before trade. It has no control rights, and it cannot acquire the target.

Its information concerns the rival's synergy class. It forms that view from its own research on the target's customers, technology, and demand.

It observes the synergy class, high synergy (\(H\)) or low synergy (\(L\)). It gets no further news about the rival's value. In the point-value example, the class fixes the value.

Neither bidder trades target shares. The informed trader stands outside the sale and earns only its trading payoff: the target shareholders' payoff per share minus the stock price on a purchase, the reverse on a sale, less order cost \(k\) on a nonzero order.
