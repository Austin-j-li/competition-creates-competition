Fixed news restores deterrence

Discussion When what the rival can learn stays fixed.

entry incumbent strength \(r\) \(r_{0}\) \(r_{1}\) \(r_{2}\) full model orders held fixed price hidden from the rival

Hold fixed the news the rival can get from the price before it prepares.

A stronger incumbent is harder to outbid, so rival gross profit falls at every belief.

\(B(\mu ;\tilde{F})\le B(\mu ;F)\text{ for every belief }\mu\)

Preparation can stop paying and can never start to pay, so entry cannot rise.

In the example, entry stays flat from the weak to the strong incumbent when orders are held fixed and when the rival cannot see the price.

With news fixed, the received answer survives. The rise in entry needs trading to change what the price tells the rival.

Fishman (1988); Hirshleifer and Png (1989).
