When two equilibria coexist

Discussion Self-confirming entry feedback against one incumbent between \(F_{0}\) and \(F_{1}\).

Uninformative market

When no informed trade is expected, rival entry stays at the floor \(e(1)=\rho\), so no order covers cost \(k\):

\(\Delta_{T}\le \dfrac{k}{\rho \max\{\pi ,\,1-\pi \}}\)
Informative market

When class \(H\) is expected to buy, a purchase lifts belief to \(\mu (1)\ge \tau\) and jumps entry to \(e(1)=1\), covering \(k\):

\(\pi <\tau \le M(\pi ),\quad \Delta_{T}\ge \dfrac{k}{1-\tau }\)
Mechanism

The discrete jump in rival entry from \(\rho\) to \(1\) at belief \(\tau\) makes informed buying self-confirming.
