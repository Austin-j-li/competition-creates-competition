Main result

A stronger incumbent can attract rival entry by making the target’s share price more informative before diligence, until auction deterrence dominates at high strength.

Future research 1Continuous cost heterogeneity

Generalising \(C\) to a continuous distribution captures rich heterogeneity in buyer preparation costs, allowing entry to respond smoothly to prices and characterising when equilibrium is globally unique across all strengths.
