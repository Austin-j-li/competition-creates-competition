Informative and uninformative markets at one strength

Proposition 3 · Paper §4.3 · Default tie rules

(b) threshold, \(\tau\) shareholder spread, \(\Delta_{T}\) \(M(1/2)\) \(\pi=1/2\) coexistence \(r_{0}\) \(r_{A}\) \(r_{F}\) \(r_{N}\)

\[\pi <\tau \le M(\pi ),\qquad \frac{k}{1-\tau }\;\le \;\Delta_{T}\;\le \;\frac{k}{\rho \max\{\pi ,1-\pi \}}\]

  • Inside this region, two markets coexist. Uninformative market: no informed trade, one price, entry \(\rho\). Informative market: only a purchase recruits, and entry is \[\rho +(1-\rho )\min\left\{ \frac{(1-\pi )\,b\,\Delta_{T}}{k},\;b+\pi \lambda \right\}\]
  • Entry in the informative market rises strictly with \(\Delta_{T}\) while the informed trader mixes on high synergy. Entry stays flat once the informed trader buys for sure on high synergy. The informative market is unique inside the region.
  • Self-confirming logic, market by market. Expect no recruitment: one order earns at most the floor return, so silence pays. Expect recruitment: a purchase lifts entry, so trade pays.

Between the Proposition 2 economies, an uninformative and an informative equilibrium can coexist at one strength.

Scope: synergy ranking, \(\Delta_{T}>0\), \(g_{H}>g_{L}\), default tie rules. In general, strengths with coexistence need not form one interval. Interval results: Backup B3, page 3.
