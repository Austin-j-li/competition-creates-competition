Received answer: strength cannot bring the rival in

  • The rival pays for diligence before learning its value.
  • Against a stronger incumbent, the rival wins less often and pays more when it wins.
  • Fishman (1988): a strong first bidder bids preemptively and deters rival diligence.

This paperThat argument assumes the rival learns nothing before diligence. Here, the rival can read the target’s share price first, and incumbent strength changes what that price reveals.

Fishman (1988); Hirshleifer and Png (1989). Auction entry: Samuelson (1985); McAfee and McMillan (1987); Levin and Smith (1994); Gentry and Stroup (2019).
