Bounded beliefs

Second building block The costly rival prepares only from the preparation threshold up.

Any order could come from the liquidity trader, so beliefs stay between \(m(\pi)\) and top belief \(M(\pi)\).

\(M(\pi)=\pi(1+u)/[\pi(1+u)+1-\pi],\quad m(\pi)=\pi/[\pi+(1-\pi)(1+u)],\quad u=\lambda/[(1-\lambda)\beta]\)

\(u\) measures informed presence relative to liquidity orders. A larger \(u\) widens the band.

The preparation threshold \(\tau\) sets rival gross profit equal to \(c_{H}\). If \(\tau>M(\pi)\), no price attracts the costly rival.

An informed order looks like an order of the liquidity trader,
so no outcome settles whether the rival has high or low synergy.
