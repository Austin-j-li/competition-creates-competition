Equilibrium in full

Tuple: \(\mathcal{E}=(\sigma_{H},\sigma_{L},\mu ,P,\bar{\mu },\zeta )\), with truthful bids. Order distribution in class \(j\): \(\sigma_{j}\) on \(\{-1,0,1\}\). Order belief: \(\mu (x)=\mathrm{Pr}(j=H\mid X=x)\). Stock price after order \(x\): \(P(x)\). Belief at a price: \(\bar{\mu }(\bar{P})=\mathrm{Pr}(j=H\mid P(X)=\bar{P})\). Preparation probability of the costly rival at price \(\bar{P}\): \(\zeta (\bar{P})\). Cost \(C=0\) prepares at each price.

Liquidity probabilities: \(\eta (-1)=\eta (1)=\beta\) and \(\eta (0)=1-2\beta\). Order probability in class \(j\): \(f_{j}(x)=(1-\lambda )\eta (x)+\lambda \sigma_{j}(x)\), with presence \(\lambda\). Entry after order \(x\): \(e(x)=\rho +(1-\rho )\zeta (P(x))\), with entry floor \(\rho\).

(E1) Order beliefs by Bayes' rule: \(\mu (x)=\pi f_{H}(x)/[\pi f_{H}(x)+(1-\pi )f_{L}(x)]\) after each order \(x\), with prior \(\pi\).

(E2) Beliefs at a price by Bayes' rule: \(\displaystyle \bar{\mu }(\bar{P})=\frac{\pi \sum_{x:\,P(x)=\bar{P}} f_{H}(x)}{\sum_{x:\,P(x)=\bar{P}} [\pi f_{H}(x)+(1-\pi )f_{L}(x)]}\) at each price \(\bar{P}\) that occurs.

(E3) Preparation: \(\zeta (\bar{P})=1\) when rival gross profit \(B(\bar{\mu }(\bar{P}))>c_{H}\), and \(\zeta (\bar{P})=0\) when \(B(\bar{\mu }(\bar{P}))<c_{H}\), with high cost \(c_{H}\). At equality, the tie rule applies.

(E4) Competitive prices: \(P(x)=\mathbb{E}[V_{T}\mid X=x]\) after each order \(x\), with the target shareholders' payoff per share \(V_{T}\). In payoff objects, \(P(x)=t_{0}+e(x)[t_{L}-t_{0}+\Delta_{T}\,\mu (x)]\), with shareholder spread \(\Delta_{T}\), class payoff \(t_{L}\) and no-entry payoff \(t_{0}\).

(E5) Orders: \(\sigma_{j}(q)>0\) only when the order \(q\) maximises the informed trader's class-\(j\) payoff \(U_{j}(q)=q[t_{0}+e(q)(t_{j}-t_{0})-P(q)]-k|q|\), with order cost \(k\). Prices \(P\) and entry \(e\) stay at the stated functions.

(E6) Truthful bids: each prepared bidder with value at or above reserve \(p\) bids its value.

Tie rules, default pair: an indifferent informed trader may mix over its best orders, and an indifferent rival at cost \(c_{H}\) prepares. Other rules: an indifferent informed trader abstains, or an indifferent rival at cost \(c_{H}\) prepares with any probability.
