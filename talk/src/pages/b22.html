Proposition 2 in exact form

Setup: the synergy distributions satisfy the class ranking (high synergy first-order dominates low synergy; each value meets the reserve \(p\)) and have finite means. The incumbent's value distribution \(F\) is continuous on \([0,\bar{r}]\), with finite upper bound \(\bar{r}\). Distributions \(F_{0}\), \(F_{1}\), \(F_{2}\) satisfy \(F_{2}\le F_{1}\le F_{0}\), so each step is a first-order stochastic strengthening. Fixed: prior \(\pi\), entry floor \(\rho\) and presence \(\lambda\), each in \((0,1)\); liquidity chance \(\beta \in (0,1/2)\); high cost \(c_{H}>0\); order cost \(k>0\). Write \(b=(1-\lambda )\beta\) for the probability of a liquidity order in one direction, and \(\kappa (\pi )=\min\{m(\pi ),1-M(\pi )\}\) for the common lower bound on the doubt, with band floor \(m(\pi )\) and top belief \(M(\pi )\).

With \(B_{i}\) rival gross profit and \(\Delta_{i}\) shareholder spread \(\Delta_{T}\) against \(F_{i}\):

(H1) \(B_{0}(\pi )<c_{H}<B_{1}(M(\pi ))\).

(H2) \(\Delta_{0}<k<\rho \,\kappa (\pi )\,\Delta_{1}\).

(H3) \(B_{2}(M(\pi ))<c_{H}\).

Weak \(F_{0}\): informed orders \((0,0)\) by synergy class (high, low); belief \(\mu (x)=\pi\) after each order \(x\); entry \(e(x)=\rho\) after each order; total entry \(\rho\); one stock price.

Strong \(F_{1}\): orders \((1,-1)\); beliefs after a sale, no order, a purchase are \((m(\pi ),\pi ,M(\pi ))\); entry is \((\rho ,\rho ,1)\); prices rise strictly; total entry is \(\rho +(1-\rho )(b+\pi \lambda )>\rho\); entry under high synergy is \(\rho +(1-\rho )(b+\lambda )\), and under low synergy \(\rho +(1-\rho )b\).

Very strong \(F_{2}\): orders and beliefs as at \(F_{1}\); entry \(\rho\) after each order; total entry \(\rho\); three distinct prices.

Ownership: \(W_{j}(F)={\mathbb{E}}_{j}[F(\theta )\mathbf{1}\{\theta \ge p\}]\) is the win chance of a prepared rival in case \(j\), where \(\theta\) is its value and the indicator \(\mathbf{1}\{\theta \ge p\}\) equals one when the value meets the reserve \(p\), and zero otherwise. High-synergy ownership rises from \(F_{0}\) to \(F_{1}\) exactly when \(e_{H}W_{H}(F_{1})>\rho \,W_{H}(F_{0})\), with \(e_{H}\) entry under high synergy at \(F_{1}\). If \(W_{H}(F_{1})=1\), high-synergy ownership rises from \(\pi \rho\) to \(\pi e_{H}\), and the high-synergy share of rival wins rises strictly exactly when \(W_{L}(F_{0})>0\).

The outcome in each economy is unique under each of the four tie-rule pairs. With the example synergy values and reserve of page 16, (H1) to (H3) hold together on an open set of inputs.
