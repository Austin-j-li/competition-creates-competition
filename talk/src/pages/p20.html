Main result

Entry rises from weak \(F_{0}\) to strong \(F_{1}\), then returns to \(\rho\) at very strong \(F_{2}\), with a unique outcome at each.

With news fixed

Holding price news fixed restores deterrence: strength can only lower entry. Higher entry comes from trading moving the price.

Extension

A wider shareholder spread raises research up to its cap, lifting entry and selecting high-synergy rivals.
