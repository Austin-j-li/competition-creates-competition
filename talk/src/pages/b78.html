Before learning \(j\), the trader covertly chooses arrival probability \(\lambda \le \bar{\lambda }\) at convex cost \((a/2)\,\lambda^{2}\).

Selection and entry

Once a purchase attracts the costly rival, wider \(\Delta_{T}\) raises \(\lambda\) up to \(\bar{\lambda }\).

Higher research lifts total entry, raising entry in class \(H\) and lowering it in class \(L\).

A wider shareholder spread raises research up to \(\bar{\boldsymbol{\lambda}}\), lifting entry and selecting high-synergy rivals.

With research too, the outcome is unique at \(r_{0}\), \(r_{1}\) and \(r_{2}\).
