Does a stronger incumbent always keep the rival out?

The incumbent is ready. The rival must pay for diligence.

Why the answer matters

Rival

Incumbent

Seller

Is a bid against a strong incumbent worth months of diligence and adviser fees? The rival pays them before it knows its value, and loses them if the incumbent wins.

Is strength alone enough to keep the rival out? With no rival, the incumbent pays only the reserve price.

Close fast with a strong incumbent, or keep the sale open for a rival? Waiting pays only if a rival pays for diligence and bids. If none does, waiting gains nothing.

The received answer is deterrence: strength can keep the rival out, but it can never bring the rival in.
