Research in full: first-order condition, branches, cap region

\[2a\lambda =\Delta_{T}m_{\lambda }-k+(\rho \Delta_{T}m_{\lambda }-k)_{+}\]

Research picks \(\lambda\) in \([0,\bar{\lambda }]\) before the slot, at cost \(a/2\lambda^{2}\) (Proposition 4)

In the example, \(a=1/40\) and the cap is \(\bar{\lambda }={}\). The marked strengths are \(r_{R}\approx{}\), \(r_{\bar{\lambda }}\approx{}\), \(r_{N}\approx{}\), \(r_{Q}\approx{}\), \(r_{C}\approx{}\)

Zero research, with entry \(\rho\), is an equilibrium exactly on \([r_{0},r_{N}]\)

Recruiting research is an equilibrium exactly on \([r_{R},r_{C}]\): research rises from to the cap on \([r_{R},r_{\bar{\lambda }})\) and stays at the cap on \([r_{\bar{\lambda }},r_{C}]\); entry rises from to and keeps that value up to \(r_{C}\)

Zero research on \([r_{0},r_{N}]\); recruiting research on \([r_{R},r_{C}]\); floor-entry research on \((r_{N},r_{Q})\) and \((r_{C},r_{2}]\), with \(r_{Q}\approx{}\).
