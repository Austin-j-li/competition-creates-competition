Weighted price impact and the winner · Proposition A.10

Let order frequency be \(\bar{f}(x)=\mathrm{Pr}(X=x)\), purchase impact \(I_{+}=P(1)-P(0)\), and sale impact \(I_{-}=P(0)-P(-1)\). Proposition A.10: under full two-sided trade, class \(H\) buys and class \(L\) sells with probability one, and prior \(\pi <\) threshold \(\tau\), the weighted difference \(\bar{f}(1)I_{+}-\bar{f}(-1)I_{-}\) is positive exactly when entry after a purchase exceeds the floor, \(e(1)>\rho\). At prior \(\pi =1/2\) the frequencies agree, and the raw impacts \(I_{+}\) and \(I_{-}\) compare directly. The entry part of the price gap \(P(1)-P(-1)\) sits in purchase impact \(I_{+}\).

Under the same full two-sided trade, where entry \(e(x)=\rho\) after each order, the order \(X\) does not predict entry. It still predicts the winner exactly when the win chance \(W_{H}\) exceeds \(W_{L}\), with \(W_{j}={\mathbb{E}}_{j}[F(\theta )\mathbf{1}\{\theta \ge p\}]\) for a prepared rival of class \(j\); the indicator \(\mathbf{1}\{\theta \ge p\}\) is one when the value meets the reserve. For a prepared rival, the win-probability gap after a purchase against a sale is \([M(\pi )-m(\pi )](W_{H}-W_{L})\).
