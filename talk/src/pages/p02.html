How a listed company is sold

Open windowAn initial bid does not close the sale: board review and shareholder votes leave weeks or months for another buyer to enter.

Due diligenceA rival cannot bid blind: to submit a binding offer it must sign an NDA, audit private records in a data room, and commit financing, which are costs lost if outbid.

Final biddingOnly prepared buyers submit binding offers; the winner acquires the company and pays out shareholders.

Stock marketTarget shares trade on the exchange throughout that window, so a prospective rival sees the live share price before paying for diligence.

Institutional evidence on pre-public M&A diligence and competition: Boone and Mulherin (2007); Gentry and Stroup (2019).
