Scope of multiplicity

As incumbent strength rises, multiple equilibria arise only in an intermediate range of \(r\). At \(F_{0}\), \(F_{1}\), and \(F_{2}\) the equilibrium is unique, so the main mechanism holds without selection.

Role of lumpy diligence cost \(\boldsymbol{C}\)

Trader information pays off only if a rival enters. With two-point cost \(C\in \{0,\,c_{H}\}\), entry jumps from \(\rho\) to \(1\) at belief \(\tau\), so at moderate \(r\) trading covers \(k\) under \(e=1\) and not under \(e=\rho\).

Why multiple equilibria can arise

M&A diligence requires lumpy upfront commitments (advisers, data-room audits, financing). Expecting an uncontested sale keeps specialists sidelined and the price flat; expecting a contest draws informed buying that pulls the rival in.

The to statement has an indifferent informed trader who may mix over its best orders, and an indifferent costly rival prepares.
