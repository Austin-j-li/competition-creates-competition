Research in full

Proposition 4 · Paper §5 · Prior \(1/2\)

One change only. Before it learns the synergy, the informed trader picks research \(\lambda \in [0,\bar{\lambda }]\) at cost \((a/2)\lambda^{2}\). Success has chance \(\lambda\), reveals the synergy, and fills the slot. Failure hands the slot to liquidity.

More research adds informed trader orders and removes liquidity cover. The prior is \(1/2\) throughout this block.

At research \(\lambda\), write \(M_{\lambda }\) for the top belief after a purchase. Recruitment intensity \(\lambda_{E}(\tau )\) solves \(M_{\lambda }=\tau\): a full purchase recruits exactly at or past this research level.

  • In the recruiting equilibrium, research \(\min\{\lambda_{R}(\Delta_{T}),\bar{\lambda }\}\) rises strictly with \(\Delta_{T}\) until the cap, then stays at the cap.
  • Entry \(\rho +(1-\rho )[\beta +(1/2-\beta )\lambda ]\) rises with research. High-synergy entry rises with research. Low-synergy entry falls.
  • The recruiting equilibrium exists exactly when \(\Delta_{T}>2k\) and \(\tau \le M_{\lambda }\) at \(\lambda =\min\{\lambda_{R}(\Delta_{T}),\bar{\lambda }\}\). Why \(2k\): a recruiting purchase gains \(\Delta_{T}m_{\lambda }\le \Delta_{T}/2\) gross, so it beats \(k\) only if \(\Delta_{T}>2k\).

A wider spread pays for more research. More research brings in a high-synergy rival more often.

Interior research \(\lambda_{R}(\Delta_{T})\) solves \(2a\lambda =\Delta_{T}m_{\lambda }-k+(\rho \Delta_{T}m_{\lambda }-k)_{+}\), with band floor \(m_{\lambda }=1-M_{\lambda }\). Branches: Backup B12, page 3. Scope: default tie rules, \(\tau >1/2\).
