0 Auction rule

Target commits to a second-price auction with reserve \(p\). Nature draws \((R,\,j,\,\theta ,\,C)\).

1 One order trades

Trader: Informed (prob. \(\lambda\)) sees \(j\), sends \(q\in \{-1,\,0,\,1\}\) at cost \(k|q|\).
Liquidity (prob. \(1-\lambda\)) sends \(-1,\,0,\,1\) with probs. \(\beta ,\,1-2\beta ,\,\beta\).

Market makers: See order \(X\), not who sent it; set \(P=E[V_{T}\mid X]\).

2 Rival decides

Rival sees only \(P\) and its cost \(C\) (knows neither \(j\), \(\theta\), nor \(X\)). Pays \(C\) to prepare, or stays out.

3 Auction

Incumbent bids \(R\); prepared rival learns \(\theta\) and bids \(\theta\). Shareholders receive \(V_{T}\); informed trader earns \(q(V_{T}-P)-k|q|\).
