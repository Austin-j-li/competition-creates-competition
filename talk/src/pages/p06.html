Related literature

Costly auction entry

Fishman (1988); Roberts and Sweeting (2013)

Holding pre-entry information fixed, a stronger opponent lowers expected auction profit and can only deter entry.

Stock-price feedback

Dow, Goldstein and Guembel (2017); Edmans, Goldstein and Jiang (2012, 2015)

Corporate insiders or acquirers act on market prices. Here, an outside rival learns about its own synergy before diligence.

Informed trading

Glosten and Milgrom (1985); Dow and Gorton (1997)

An outside specialist trades against liquidity flow, and market makers price the order alongside the entry it induces.

ContributionI connect costly auction entry to learning from the share price, showing how incumbent strength moves shareholder proceeds and rival profit in opposite directions.
