Payoffs and Proposition 1 in full

Proposition 1 · Paper §3.1

Proposition 1 Let \(\tilde{F}\le F\) be a first-order stochastic strengthening of the distribution \(F\).

(a) For any class distributions with finite means, gross auction profit \(g_{H}\) and \(g_{L}\) weakly fall. Rival profit \(B(\mu )\) weakly falls at each fixed belief \(\mu\).

(b) Under the class ranking \(G_{H}\le G_{L}\), the shareholder spread \(\Delta_{T}\) weakly rises.

Under the class ranking, \(\Delta_{T}(F)+g_{H}(F)-g_{L}(F)={\mathbb{E}}_{H}[\max\{\theta ,p\}]-{\mathbb{E}}_{L}[\max\{\theta ,p\}]\), with the rival's value \(\theta\) and reserve \(p\).

Intuition: one class value gap is fixed, and strength moves its split between the target shareholders' payoff and rival gross profit.
