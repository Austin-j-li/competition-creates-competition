A stronger incumbent can bring the rival in

The rival must pay for diligence before knowing its synergy, but some traders already know something about it.

  1. 1Incumbent strength is public.
  2. →2Observing a strong incumbent, informed traders start buying.
  3. →3Informed buying pushes the target stock price up.
  4. →4A price rise signals high synergy.
  5. →5The rival may then pay for diligence.

A stronger incumbent is still harder for the rival to outbid.

Where it stopsIf the incumbent is too strong, even a price rise in the target share is not enough. Entry can first rise with strength, then fall back.
