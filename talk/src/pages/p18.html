Research is the informed trader choosing \(\lambda\) up to a cap \(\bar{\lambda }\) before any order is sent. Market makers and the rival do not see the chosen \(\lambda\).

\(\text{cost }(a/2)\,\lambda^{2},\quad 0\le \lambda \le \bar{\lambda }\)

In the equilibrium where a purchase attracts the costly rival, research rises with the shareholder spread up to the cap.

Where research rises, entry by high-synergy rivals rises and by low-synergy rivals falls. Prepared rivals are more often high-synergy.

\(\text{entry }=\rho +(1-\rho )\,[\beta +(1/2-\beta )\,\lambda ]\)

With research too, the outcome is unique at \(r_{0}\), \(r_{1}\) and \(r_{2}\).

A wider shareholder spread raises research up to the cap,
and higher research raises entry through high-synergy rivals.
